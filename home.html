    Navkar Mantra

    The supreme salutation of Jainism

    णमो अरिहंताणं

    णमो सिद्धाणं

    णमो आयरियाणं

    णमो उवज्झायाणं

    णमो लोए सव्वसाहूणं

    एसो पंच णमुक्कारो, सव्व पावप्पणासणो।
    मंगलाणं च सव्वेसिं, पढमं हवइ मंगलं॥

    Namo Arihantanam · Namo Siddhanam · Namo Ayariyanam · Namo Uvajjhayanam · Namo Loe Savva-sahunam

    I bow to the Arihants, who have conquered their inner enemies; to the Siddhas, the liberated souls; to the Acharyas, the spiritual guides; to the Upadhyayas, the teachers; and to all Sadhus of the world. This fivefold salutation destroys all sins, and of all that is auspicious it is the first.

    What is Jainism?

    Jainism is an ancient path of non-violence, truth and self-control. It teaches that every living being has a soul and deserves respect. Jains aim to live lightly, speak kindly and free the soul from karma, step by step. You do not need to know any Sanskrit or Prakrit to begin.

    Five principles

    Ahimsa

    Do no harm in thought, word or deed.

    Satya

    Speak the truth, kindly.

    Asteya

    Take only what is freely given.

    Brahmacharya

    Practice restraint and purity.

    Aparigraha

    Hold few possessions, and hold them loosely.
